## How to use this log

**Adding a new decision:** Increment the number, add a row at the top of the table, fill all four columns. Be specific about what was rejected and why — the rejected alternatives are often more useful than the decision itself when you need to defend your choices.

**Superseding a decision:** Add the new decision as a new row at the top. In the old row's rationale column, add "⚠ Superseded by Decision #N." Do not delete the old row.

**Using this in your thesis:** The rationale column maps directly to your methodology section. "We chose X over Y because Z" is already written here. The alternatives considered column is your defense against reviewer questions about why you didn't do it differently.
